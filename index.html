How Vespera trains you

Four habits of a good coach, built into the board.

Every part of Vespera exists to answer one question after every move: was that good, and if not, what should you have played instead?

Humanlike play

An opponent that misses things too.

Slide the strength dial from a shaky beginner to a sharp club player. Vespera doesn't calculate flawlessly — it plays the moves a person at that level would actually find, mistakes included.

That means real practice: openings that get met with normal replies, not engine refutations on move six.

Move grading

Every move, graded the moment you play it.

Best, good, inaccuracy, mistake, or blunder — Vespera compares what you played against what was available and tells you instantly, without breaking the flow of the game.
